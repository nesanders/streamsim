StreamSim is a market simulation: 1,000 virtual viewers each independently decide every month whether to subscribe to or cancel streaming services, based on price, content quality, genre preferences, and the economy. Your job is to adjust the controls on the left and run the simulation — then observe how individual decisions add up to market outcomes. Use Scenarios for guided activities, or How It Works to see the math behind the model.
