StreamSim is a market simulation: 1,000 virtual viewers each independently decide every month whether to subscribe to or cancel streaming services, based on price, content quality, genre preferences, and the economy. Your job is to adjust the controls in the Sim Params panel and run the simulation — then observe how individual decisions add up to market outcomes. Use Scenarios for guided activities, or How It Works to see the math behind the model.

Service Subscription Correlations

How much do subscriber counts move together? +1 = in sync, −1 = opposite, 0 = no relationship. Raw counts are often all high because every service tends to grow early on — that shared trend inflates correlations. Check "Use monthly changes" to remove the trend and see true co-movement.
